Privacy Policy

Bluebird Events app · Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bluebird Events app (“the app”) collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. The app is provided by Bluebird Events Pvt. Ltd. (“Bluebird”, “we”, “us”), CT-2, 202, Taj Nagri, Amar Lok Colony, Phase II, Tajganj, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282001, India. If you have any question about this policy, write to bluebirdeventsindia@gmail.com.

1. What the app is for

The app helps people attend conferences run by Bluebird and its event partners. You join an event with a code, then see its schedule, venue, announcements, nearby recommendations, photo gallery, and chat with other attendees and organizers.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhen and why
Name, email address and passwordWhen you create an account. Used to sign you in and to send your confirmation and password-reset codes. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never visible to us in readable form.
Mobile phone numberWhen you create an account. Used so event organizers can reach you during an event. It is not shown to other attendees.
Profile details (job title, company, short bio, interests, profile photo)Only if you choose to add them. Shown to other people at events you have joined so you can network.
Event activity (events you join, sessions you save to your agenda, session ratings)To give you your personal agenda and to give organizers feedback on sessions.
Messages you send in event chatTo deliver them to the people in that channel or conversation.
Photos you upload to an event gallery, and floor plans or images organizers uploadTo show them to the other members of that event. The app only accesses photos you choose to share.

What we do not collect. The app does not access your device’s location, contacts, microphone or camera roll in the background. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your information.

3. Notifications

If you save a session and allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your own phone shortly before the session starts. These reminders are created and stored on your device. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings at any time.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other attendees of an event you joined can see your name, and any title, company, bio, interests and photo you added. They can see messages you post in shared channels and photos you add to the gallery.
  • Event organizers can see who has joined their event, and can manage that event’s content and members.
  • The Bluebird team can see your phone number and manage events on the platform.
  • Direct messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.

Access is enforced in our database, so people outside an event cannot read that event’s attendee list, chat or gallery.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is open. Event content such as chat messages and gallery photos stays with that event, and may be deleted by the organizers or by us when the event is removed. When you ask us to delete your account (see section 7), we delete your profile and personal details; messages and photos you posted are deleted or made anonymous.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your details any time from More → Edit my profile.
  • Leave an event from within the app.
  • Delete your account and data. In the app, go to More → Your profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, phone number and messages, and takes you out of every conference, straight away. If you cannot open the app, email bluebirdeventsindia@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”, and we will act on it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by writing to the same address.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults attending professional events. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
